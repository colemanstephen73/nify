# 03 · Track Session Cards

**One card = one track day.** Morning: runs 1–4 plus a choice run. Afternoon: the
**twist** (same focus, done a bit differently) plus a choice run. That's up to 10
runs, and **stopping early is always allowed.**

Each card has:
- **one focus**
- **max 3 cues** (main + 2 back-ups, from `06`)
- a **picture strip** to show your child (they can't read yet, so point and say it)
- a paddock warm-up
- the runs, each with a drill and what success looks like
- **ask-first** debrief questions between runs (`07`)
- the score to log (`00` item)
- links to the sim card and the next card

Drills are in `05-drill-library.md`. Check `09-safety.md` before every day.

---

## Read once: the parts every card shares

### Before run 1 (every day)
1. Safety check (`09`). Cones out only if the track allows (`05`).
2. Quiet foot routine: helmet on, hands on the wheel, **T5 "quiet foot in the pits"** until the helper says "go". The Comer C52 has a **centrifugal clutch** [S24]: the kart moves as soon as the revs rise.
3. **Sunday only:** run 1 repeats **yesterday's main cue** as a warm-up run.

**Keeping to 3 cues:** T5 is a safety routine said the same way every day, so it
doesn't count toward the 3. On Sundays, yesterday's cue **does** count. Use it, the
card's main cue, and at most one back-up. [Synthesis]

### The "Your Choice" run (runs 5 and 10)
Show your child the choice pictures. They point to one. [S2]

| Picture | Run |
|---|---|
| 🐢 | **Snail & wave:** one slow lap waving at you, then normal driving |
| 🎯 | **Pirate target:** you hold up a colour at the fence, they tell you after (D05) |
| 🔁 | **Again!:** repeat their favourite drill from today |
| 🆓 | **Free run:** just drive. No cue, no drill, no questions after. |

### Between every run
- **Your child talks first.** Ask before telling. [Your brief; Synthesis]
- Use the photo map from the track walk (D16). Your child **points** instead of explaining.
- **One** comment from you at most, using **today's cue words only**.
- Water, snack, shade. 5+ minutes of rest. Engine-off games are fine.

### End of the day
1. End on a **good run**. If the last run went badly, do one short 🐢 run.
2. **Fun face:** your child points to 😞 1 · 🙁 2 · 😐 3 · 🙂 4 · 🤩 5
3. "What was the best bit?" Write it in the tracker **in their words**.
4. Log the score(s). Check the fun rule.

## TR-00 · Fun Only Day (the back-off card)

**Use when:** fun dropped two sessions in a row, after a scary moment, or whenever
you feel karting is becoming a chore. [S4, Synthesis]

**Picture strip:** 🎉 🏎️ 🐢 🆓 🍦
**Focus:** enjoy driving. **Cues:** none. Really, none.

| Run | What | Success looks like |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | 🆓 Free runs | Smiling at the pit gate |
| 4 | 🐢 Snail & wave | Waves at you |
| 5+ | Child picks from the choice pictures, **or stops** | They decide |

- **Max 6 runs.** Stopping after 2 is fine.
- **No debrief questions.** Only: "Did you have fun?" and "What shall we have for lunch?"
- Plan a non-karting treat afterwards.
- **Log:** session_type `track`, focus `Fun Only`, score blank, fun face.
- **Next:** repeat the last card your child *enjoyed*.

| Run | Drill | Cue | Success looks like |
|---|---|---|---|

| 3 | D03, slightly quicker leader | R1 | Eyes forward, not checking behind |
| 4 | Free run: "Show me Dinosaur Corner!" | V1 | Smiles, smooth |
| 5 | **Your Choice** | — | — |

for 2 laps, and your child copies. Then free running. Run 10 = Your Choice.

**Debrief questions (pick 1–2):**
- "Point to your favourite corner on the photos."
- "Was it easy or tricky to follow me?" (thumb up / sideways / down)
- "What colour was the kerb at Dinosaur Corner?"

**Score:** baseline for all of section A–C in `00` (watch only). Log D03 score 1–3.

## TR-02 · Sponge & Toothpaste (smooth pedals)

**Picture strip:** 🧽 (brake) · 🪥 (go) · 👣 (feet take turns)
**Focus:** smooth pedals: squeeze on, squeeze off, one at a time.
**Cues:** **T1** "Squeeze the go-pedal like toothpaste" · B1 "Squeeze the brake like a sponge" · T4 "Feet take turns"
**Drills:** D01 Paddock Pedal Game · G10 Sponge Squeeze (warm-up)
**Kit:** a real sponge and a cup of water (G10), picture cards 🧽 🪥

**Warm-up (5 min):** G10, squeeze a real wet sponge slowly into a cup ("make it rain slowly") → D01 in the kart, engine off.

| Run | Drill | Cue | Success looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | *(Sunday)* yesterday's cue: R1 | R1 | Eyes forward |
| 2 | "Toothpaste out of the corners": smooth throttle as the kart leaves each corner | T1 | Engine note rises smoothly, no stab-stab |
| 3 | "Sponge before the corners": gentle squeeze where your child needs to slow | B1 | No skids, kart doesn't lurch |
| 4 | Both, "feet take turns" | T4 | Never both pedals together (watch the kart pitch) |
| 5 | **Your Choice** | — | — |

**Afternoon twist:** D01 again in the paddock with **eyes closed** (you call "sponge!" / "toothpaste!"), then runs 6–9 repeating runs 2–4 in any order **your child picks**.

**Debrief questions:**
- "Was your toothpaste slow or fast?"
- "Show me with your hand how you pressed the sponge." (they squeeze)
- "Which corner had the smoothest toothpaste?" (point on the photos)

**Score:** A02, A03, A04 (1–3).
**Links:** no sim tonight (Sunday). Next: **TR-03** (next Saturday).
