# 08 · Off-Track: Movement, Coordination, Reaction, Multi-Sport

## Why this matters as much as track time

- At 5, your child is in **Active Start** (birth to 6). The aim is **confidence and joy in
  movement**, through a mix of structured and unstructured play. From 6, **FUNdamentals**
  is about 75% general activity and 25% sport-specific. [S29]
- Adult **world-class** athletes did **more different sports** as children, and less
  of their main sport, than national-level athletes. [S30]
- WHO: children aged 5–17 should average **60 minutes a day** of moderate-to-vigorous
  activity, with vigorous play and muscle and bone strengthening on 3 days a week. [S34]

So: **karting is one sport among many.** Weekdays belong to play, other sports
and the games below. [Synthesis]

## How to use the games

- **Track days:** use them as the **warm-up** (5 min) and as **between-run** games in the paddock (engine off).
- **Weekdays:** optional, only if your child asks. 5–10 minutes. Make it play, not training.
- Each game says which **karting skill** it links to, so you can say:
  "That's what your hands do in the kart!" [Synthesis]
- **Rule:** if it stops being fun, stop or switch games.

## Reaction games

### G01 · Red Light, Green Light
- **Links to:** pedals, stopping (B2, T2), flags
- **How:** you call (or show a card) 🟩 "green", and they run or "drive". 🟥 "red", they freeze. Add 🟨 "yellow" for slow motion.
- **Make it harder:** use only cards (no voice) · switch faster · your child is the caller.

### G02 · Flag Statues
- **Links to:** D04, R2
- **How:** your child "drives" around the paddock with a paper-plate wheel (G12). You show a flag: 🟩 fast, 🟨 slow snail, 🟥 statue, 🏁 finish party dance.
- **Make it harder:** add your track's other flags when the 4 basic ones are easy.

### G04 · Ball Drop Catch
- **Links to:** reaction, hand-eye, "same every time" (D11)
- **How:** hold a tennis ball at your child's shoulder height. Drop it without warning. They catch it **after one bounce**.
- **Make it harder:** catch before it bounces · drop from different heights · two balls, you say which one.

## Hand-eye coordination

### G03 · Partner Toss
- **Links to:** hand-eye, two-hand coordination
- **How:** gentle underarm toss of a beanbag or soft ball, 2–3 big steps apart. Count catches in a row.
- **Make it harder:** one hand · step back after 5 catches · toss to the left hand, then the right.

### G11 · Mirror Me
- **Links to:** calm hands (D15, W1–W3)
- **How:** face each other. Move your hands slowly like a steering wheel. Your child copies like a mirror. Slow and smooth.
- **Make it harder:** your child leads · close eyes and "feel" a slow turn on a real plate.

### G10 · Sponge Squeeze
- **Links to:** B1, T1 (pedal smoothness)
- **How:** a wet sponge over a cup. "Make it rain slowly… now a storm… now slowly again." Then try with a foot (sponge on the floor, bare foot squeeze, outside or in the bath).
- **Make it harder:** fill the cup to a line you drew.

## Balance and body control

### G05 · One-Foot Pirate
- **Links to:** balance, core (sitting steady in a bumpy kart)
- **How:** stand on one foot like a pirate with a wooden leg. Count together. Swap feet.
- **Make it harder:** eyes closed (stand next to you) · on a cushion · catch a ball (G03) on one foot.

### G06 · Tightrope Walk
- **Links to:** balance, eyes up (V1)
- **How:** walk along a line of tape, a garden hose, or a low kerb. **Look at the end, not your feet.**
- **Make it harder:** backwards · carry a cup of water · you hold up fingers at the end for them to count while walking (eyes up).

### G13 · Animal Walks
- **Links to:** whole-body strength (arms and shoulders help with steering)
- **How:** bear crawl 🐻, crab walk 🦀, frog jumps 🐸, inchworm 🐛 across the garden.
- **Make it harder:** races · an animal obstacle course your child designs.

## Steering and vision

### G07 · Look-Far Game
- **Links to:** V1, D05
- **How:** stand on one side of a park or the paddock. You hold up coloured cards or fingers far away. Your child calls them out.
- **Make it harder:** your child is moving (walking, scooting) while calling.

### G08 · Scooter or Balance-Bike Slalom
- **Links to:** V2, V5 (look at the *next* cone), W3 (smooth arcs)
- **How:** 4–6 cones in a wavy line. Ride through: "look at the **next** cone, not this one."
- **Make it harder:** wider or tighter spacing · one hand on the bars (only if safe) · you call "whale / fish / crab" lines (D12).
- **Safety:** helmet on for scooters and bikes.

### G09 · Shadow Tag
- **Links to:** R3 (holding a line in traffic)
- **How:** your child walks a set path (a chalk line or the edge of a path). You run past on either side. They keep to their path, "a train on its track".
- **Make it harder:** jog instead of walk · two adults run past.

### G12 · Paper-Plate Driver
- **Links to:** D03 (on-foot version), D12, track walks
- **How:** your child holds a paper plate as a steering wheel. They follow you along "the line" (a chalk track, a garden track, or the real track when closed). Hands at 9 and 3 (W2).
- **Make it harder:** your child leads and you follow · draw a kid-size track with chalk and "drive" whale, fish and crab lines.

## Multi-sport ideas (pick what your child enjoys) [Synthesis]

| Activity | What it builds |
|---|---|
| Swimming | Water safety, whole-body fitness |
| Balance bike or cycling | Balance, steering, looking ahead |
| Gymnastics or tumbling | Body control, spatial awareness, strength |
| Ball sports (soccer, T-ball, mini-tennis) | Hand-eye, foot-eye, reaction, team play |
| Climbing (playground or kids' wall) | Grip, strength, confidence |
| Dance or martial arts | Rhythm, balance, following movement patterns |
| Unstructured outdoor play | Everything, and it's what this age is built for [S29] |

**Sleep and screens:** at 3–5, children need 10–13 hours of sleep per 24 hours (including naps) [S33].
Sim and other screens must not push into sleep or active play. [S31]
