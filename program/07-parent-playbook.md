# 07 · Parent Playbook

You are coach **and** parent. Your child will remember how karting *felt* long
before they remember lap times. These scripts keep the feel right.

**Three rules** [Your brief; Synthesis]
1. **No results talk before driving.** Never "let's get top 5" or "beat Jack today".
2. **Nothing new on the grid.** Last-minute advice crowds a small brain.
3. **Ask before you tell.** Your child talks first, every time. End on a positive, every day.

Words in quotes are scripts. Use them as they are, or in your own words.

---

## Before the day (car or breakfast)

- "What are you most excited about today?"
- "Shall we find treasures on the track walk?"
- Show **today's picture strip** from the card (`03`): "Today we're pirates!" 🏴‍☠️🔭

**Don't:** talk about times, other kids, or "trying hard". Don't run through the whole plan.

## In the paddock, before run 1

- Do the warm-up game (it's on the card). Laugh.
- Show the **one** main cue with its hand sign (`06`). Have your child do the sign back.
- "Quiet foot in the pits, then off you go!" (T5)

## On the grid or at the pit exit (final seconds)

Say **only one** of these:
- "Have fun, I'm watching you!"
- "I love watching you drive."
- Thumbs up 👍 + the cue's hand sign.

**Don't:** give new instructions, fix the helmet strap for the fifth time (check it earlier), or show worry on your face.

---

## Between runs (the debrief)

### The 4-step debrief (2–3 minutes) [Synthesis, built on S2]
1. **Care first:** water, "Are you warm or cold?", visor up, kart shade.
2. **They talk first (open question):**
   - "Tell me about that run!"
   - "Which corner felt best?" (they point at the photo map)
   - "Thumb up, sideways, or down?" 👍 👊 👎
3. **One choice question about today's focus** (from the card), e.g.
   - "Was your toothpaste slow or fast?"
   - "Did you wait for the cone?"
4. **One small thing from you, if needed, in cue words only:**
   - "Next run: pirate telescope." (V1 sign)
   - …or nothing, if it's going well. Praise works: "I saw the baby bird hands at Dinosaur Corner!"

### Good praise (specific, about effort and actions) [Synthesis]
- "Your foot stayed on the floor all the way to the cone!"
- "You waited for the turn every lap."
- "You kept going after that wobble. That's a racing driver."

### Less helpful praise
- "You're a natural." (talent, not action)
- "Fastest lap!" (result, not action; it makes speed the goal)

---

## After the day (in the car or at dinner)

- "What was the best bit?" (write it in the tracker, in their words)
- "Point to a face: how much fun was today?" 😞🙁😐🙂🤩
- "What shall we do on the sim tonight / next weekend?" (choice)
- Then **stop talking about karting** unless your child brings it up.

---

## Things not to say (and what to say instead) [Synthesis]

| Instead of… | Try… |
|---|---|
| "Let's get a top 5 today." | "Let's find out what the pirate sees today!" |
| "Why did you do that?" | "Tell me about that corner." |
| "Don't crash." | "Look at the gap." (V4) |
| "You're slower than last week." | Nothing. Or: "Which run felt best?" |
| "Look at how fast Max is." | Never compare with other kids. |
| "Try harder." | One cue: "Foot to the floor until the cone." |
| "You were great, but…" | "You were great at ___." (stop there) |
| "We paid a lot for this day." | Never connect money to your child's driving. |

## Crashes and spins

### Your own response (first 30 seconds)
1. **Stay where you are.** Never run onto the track. Marshals handle it. [Synthesis; follow your track's rules]
2. **Watch for the hand up** (R5). Breathe. Keep your face calm, because your child will look for it.
3. When your child is back with you: **body first, feelings second, kart last.**

### Script
- "Are you OK? Does anything hurt?" (check properly, see `09`)
- "That was a big spin! How did it feel?" *(let them say "scary", "funny", anything)*
- "You did the hand-up just like we practised. That was perfect."
- "Do you want to go back out, or have a snack first?" **(their choice)**

**After a big hit:** if there's any knock to the head, dizziness, headache, confusion or
being "not themselves", **stop for the day and get medical advice.** Never "walk it off". [Synthesis; see `09`]

## Fear

Fear is normal and useful. It means your child's brain is paying attention. [Synthesis]

- **Name it, don't fight it:** "It felt fast, didn't it? That's OK."
- **Shrink the task:** move the cone back, do a 🐢 snail run, or follow the leader (D03).
- **Give control:** "You decide when we move the cone." (TR-07 always lets your child choose.)
- **Never** say "there's nothing to be scared of" or push one more run.
- If fear stays for a whole day → next session is **TR-00**.

## Bad days

Signs: tears, "I don't want to", slow or sloppy runs, looking for you at every lap.

1. **End the drill.** Do one 🐢 or 🆓 run, then stop.
2. "Some days are like this, even for champions. Let's get ice cream."
3. Log it honestly (fun face). If fun drops two sessions in a row → **TR-00** next time.
4. **Look at yourself too:** was the day too long, too hot, too many runs, too much talking? [Synthesis]

## When your child wants to stop karting

Listen. Ask: "What's not fun about it?" Often it's one fixable thing: the noise,
cold hands, the helmet, too much waiting, or too much coaching. Fix that. If they
still want a break, **take one.** Other sports build the same athlete. [S30]

---

## Race days or club events (later, only if your child wants)

- **Before:** same as a normal day. "Have fun and drive your laps." No positions.
- **On the grid:** "I love watching you." Nothing else.
- **After, win or lose:** "I loved watching you race. What was the most fun bit?"
- **The car ride home is quiet** unless your child wants to talk.

## Sim time

- **Use the sand timer.** "When the sand's gone, the sim goes to sleep." [Synthesis]
- **Sit with them** (AAP: share screen time at this age [S31]).
- **No leaderboards, no "fastest lap".** [S16 ⚠️]
- If the sim causes fights at bedtime, move it earlier or skip it. Track and sleep matter more. [S31, S33]
