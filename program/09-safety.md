# 09 · Safety Checklists

Print this. Tick it **every** track day and before the first sim session.
**Your club's and series' rules always come first.** Kid Kart rules differ by series [S26],
so ask your track or club for their current rulebook and follow it over anything here.

> **Non-negotiables**
> 1. **Brakes are never disabled, restricted or weakened**, for any drill or reason.
> 2. **An adult is always present**: at the track (in the allowed area) and next to the sim.
> 3. **If in doubt, don't run.** Sit out a run, or a day.

## A · Kart check (Kid Kart + Comer C52), before the first run

Use your kart's manual and your club's tech sheet. This is a reminder list, not a full tech inspection. [Synthesis]

| ✓ | Item | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| ☐ | **Brakes** | Pedal firm, kart doesn't roll when pushed with the brake on, no fluid leaks or loose cables/rods |
| ☐ | **Throttle** | Springs back to idle **by itself** every time. Throttle stop set as your series requires |
| ☐ | **Kill switch** | The C52 has an integrated kill switch [S24]. Test that it stops the engine, and that **you** know where it is |
| ☐ | **Chain guard** | Fitted and secure. Small fingers + chain = serious injury risk |
| ☐ | **Clutch** | Centrifugal clutch [S24]: the kart creeps if idle is too high. Idle set so the kart **doesn't move** at idle |
| ☐ | **Engine** | Stock as supplied, if your series requires it (WKA requires a stock C52 [S25]). No fuel leaks, fuel cap tight |
| ☐ | **Wheels and tyres** | Nuts tight, pressures as advised, no cuts |
| ☐ | **Steering** | No play or binding, all bolts and cotter pins in place |
| ☐ | **Bodywork and bumpers** | Nerf bars, nose and rear bumper fitted and tight |
| ☐ | **Seat fit** | Child sits snug, can reach full brake and full throttle with a slight knee bend, and can turn the wheel lock to lock without stretching |
| ☐ | **Ballast** | Any weights bolted (double-nutted or as your series requires), never just taped |
| ☐ | **Loose items** | Nothing loose in the kart, and no long hair, scarves or cords that could reach moving parts |

### After any crash or hard hit [Synthesis; follow your track's medical rules]
- Let **marshals** reach your child first. Don't run onto the track.
- Check: pain, how they move, how they talk and act.
- **Any head knock with headache, dizziness, confusion, sickness, sleepiness or "not themselves" → stop for the day and get medical advice.**
- Check the helmet. A helmet that took a hard hit may need replacing even if it looks fine.
- Check the kart (A) before running again.

## D · Sim rig: before the first session (do this without your child)

Direct-drive wheels can spin hard and fast. Fanatec has published no settings for children.
**Every number below is a [Synthesis] starting point.** Go lower if you're unsure.

### Rig 1 · Force feedback (torque) on the Fanatec wheelbase
- **FF** in the Fanatec tuning menu sets the base's **maximum torque**, whatever the game's settings are [S22].
  For example, at 100% a CSL DD (with Boost Kit) can give up to 8 Nm and a DD2 up to 25 Nm [S22].
- **Starting point [Synthesis]:** aim for a **peak of about 1 Nm or less** at first.
  Work out FF % = 1 ÷ your base's rated maximum Nm (check your manual for the exact rating). For example:
  - **8 Nm base** (e.g. CSL DD with Boost Kit [S22]): FF ≈ 10–12%
  - **20 Nm base** (e.g. DD1 [S22]): FF ≈ 5%
  - **25 Nm base** (e.g. DD2 [S22]): FF ≈ 4%
  - **Belt-driven bases** are gentler. Start around 20% and use the tests below.
- Only raise it in small steps if the tests below are easy **and** your child says the wheel feels "floppy".
- **Game force feedback:** set it low too, and turn off "heavy" effects (big kerb or crash jolts) if the sim allows.
- **Adult test (do it yourself first):**
  1. Drive a lap and hit a wall on purpose. The wheel should **never** feel like it could hurt a wrist or thumb.
  2. Let go of the wheel mid-corner. If it spins or shakes hard, **lower FF**, or raise the damping (Fanatec's tuning menu has damper settings; see the FAQ [S23]).
- **Child test:** your child should be able to **hold the wheel still with relaxed "baby bird" hands** when it pushes. If they can't, lower FF.
- **Write your final settings here:** FF: ____ % · Game FFB: ____ · Damper: ____ · Date: ____
- **Profiles:** if your base supports saved profiles, save a "KID" profile and check it's selected **every session**.

### Rig 2 · Hands and wheel
- **Fingers outside the wheel rim and spokes.** No thumbs hooked inside the spokes. [Your brief; Synthesis]
- **"Hands off = pause."** Teach it. Practise it in SIM-01.
- **You can reach the power switch** (or the wall plug) in one move. Practise turning it off.
- A smaller wheel rim suits small hands, if you have one. Check the quick release is locked.

### Rig 3 · Pedals
- Set the pedals up **like the Kid Kart: right foot go, left foot brake.** Remove the clutch pedal, or move it out of reach. [Synthesis]
- **Brake force (BRF):** Fanatec's load-cell brakes need force to reach 100% (default BRF = 50). **Lower BRF** means less force is needed [S23].
  Set it so your child reaches **full brake with a firm squeeze** (check the brake level readout), but not with a light tap. A light tap would teach on-off braking.
- Pedals must not slide. Fix the pedal plate firmly to the rig.

### Rig 4 · Seat and fit
- Use a booster cushion, a seat insert, or move the seat so your child:
  - reaches **full** throttle and brake with a slight knee bend
  - holds the wheel at 9 and 3 with slightly bent elbows
  - has eyes level with the **middle of the centre screen**
- Pedal extenders or a foot box if their legs can't reach. Everything bolted or strapped, nothing balanced.
- **Triple monitors:** stands or arms tight. Nothing a child could pull over. Cables tidied away from feet. [Synthesis]

### Rig 5 · Every sim session
| ✓ | Item |
|---|---|
| ☐ | Adult sitting next to the rig the whole time |
| ☐ | "KID" torque profile checked (Rig 1) |
| ☐ | Fingers outside the spokes, "hands off = pause" reminded |
| ☐ | Timer set: 10–15 min (`04`) |
| ☐ | At least 1 hour before bed [S31] |
| ☐ | Room lit, screens at a comfortable brightness |
| ☐ | Stop if your child is upset, tired, or says their eyes, wrists or head hurt |

## E · Supervision summary

| Situation | Adult role |
|---|---|
| Track runs | Watching from the allowed area, phone in hand, knows the kill switch and the marshal post |
| Paddock | Child never near a running engine or a hot kart without you. Kart on a stand with the engine off for D01 |
| Cones | You place them, only when allowed and no karts are moving |
| Sim | Next to the rig, hand near the power switch |
| Video and drone | Within track rules and local law |
