# Start Here: Week 1

**One page. Read it Friday night.** The details are in the other files.

## Friday night (30 min, without your child)
- [ ] **Sim set-up** (`09` section D): set Fanatec **FF to about 1 Nm peak** (e.g. ~10% on an 8 Nm base), lower **BRF** until a firm squeeze gives full brake, right foot go / left foot brake, clutch pedal away, seat and pedal fit. **Test it yourself first.** Save a "KID" profile.
- [ ] Install **Automobilista 2** and pick one short kart track + the slowest **rental kart**. **No iRacing** (13+ only).
- [ ] **Ask your track:** can we use soft cones? Can an adult or older driver lead my child? What's the rule after a spin?
- [ ] Pack: gear (`09` B), 4–6 soft cones, phone, paper plate, sponge, a cup, flag cards (🟩🟨🟥🏁), stickers, snacks, water, sand timer.
- [ ] Print: `09` checklists, **TR-01** and **TR-02** (`03`), the weekend planner (`02`), the cue signs **R1, V1, T5, T1, B1, T4** (`06`).

## Saturday: TR-01 "Hello Kart"
1. **Kart + gear check** (`09` A, B).
2. **Track walk treasure hunt** (D16): name the corners ("Dinosaur Corner"), photo each one.
3. **Warm-up:** G12 paper-plate "walk the line" behind you.
4. **Runs 1–4:** free run (you just **watch and fill in the baseline**, `00`), then **follow the leader** (D03). Cue: **R1 "Your lap is in front of you."**
5. **Run 5: Your Choice** 🐢 🎯 🔁 🆓
6. Lunch + 45 min free play.
7. **Afternoon twist (runs 6–10):** the leader drives a slightly different line, your child copies, then free runs. Stop early if attention fades.
8. **End on a good run → fun face (1–5) → "What was the best bit?"**

**Evening: SIM-01 (15 min max, ≥1 hr before bed, sitting with them).** Fit check → pedal game 🧽🪥 → 5 min free drive while you **watch for sim habits** (walls, grass, restarts).

## Sunday: TR-02 "Sponge & Toothpaste"
1. Checks. Warm-up: **G10** (squeeze a wet sponge slowly) → **D01** pedal game in the kart, engine off.
2. **Run 1:** yesterday's cue (R1).
3. **Runs 2–4:** toothpaste throttle out of corners (**T1**) → sponge brake before corners (**B1**) → "feet take turns" (**T4**).
4. **Run 5: Your Choice.** Lunch + play. **Twist:** D01 with eyes closed, then your child picks the order of runs 6–9.
5. End on a win → fun face → log it.

## The 6 rules to remember all season
1. **One focus, max 3 cues.** Same words every time.
2. **They talk first.** "Tell me about that run!" Point at the photo map.
3. **Nothing new on the grid.** "Have fun, I'm watching you!"
4. **A child's choice in every session.**
5. **Fun drops 2 sessions in a row → TR-00 Fun Only** next time.
6. **Move on by skill, not by date.** Gate after Week 1: A02 and A03 at 2 or more (`00`).
