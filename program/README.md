# Youth Karting Driver Development Program

**Driver:** age 5 · Kid Kart with Comer C52 engine · 1 day of track experience · lots of sim experience
**Coach:** you (parent) · **Schedule:** weekends only, both days, up to 8–10 runs a day
**Sim:** Fanatec wheel and pedals, triple monitors (PC assumed)
**Child can't read yet:** cards use pictures and emoji, and you read the words aloud.

> 👉 New here? Read [`START-HERE.md`](START-HERE.md) first. It's one page.

---

## 1. The big idea

Your child is 5. At this age the job is **fun, confidence, and a kart that feels
easy to drive**. Speed comes later, on its own.

Five rules run through every file:

1. **One focus per session. At most 3 cues.** A cue is a short phrase you say,
   like "squeeze like a sponge". [Synthesis, built on S1, S2]
2. **Talk about the world, not the body.** Say "aim for the cone", not
   "bend your elbow". Children learn better from pictures in their head
   (analogies) and from targets outside their body. [S1, S2]
3. **The child gets a choice every session.** A warm-up game, which run to do
   again, or the last run of the day. Choice helps motivation and learning. [S2]
4. **Move on when the skill shows up, not when the calendar says.** Each phase has
   exit criteria. Weeks are only a guide. [Synthesis]
5. **Fun is measured.** After every session your child points to a face (1–5).
   **If the fun rating drops two sessions in a row, the next session is a
   "Fun Only" day** (card `TR-00`). [Synthesis]

> **What "drops two sessions in a row" means:** session B is lower than session A,
> *and* session C is lower than B. Example: 4 → 3 → 2.
> Also: **two sessions in a row rated 1 or 2** count as a drop.
> The tracker (`/tracker/tracker.html`) checks this for you.

## 2. Why the plan is shaped like this

| Fact | What we do with it |
|---|---|
| At 5, your child is in the **Active Start** stage (birth to 6). The goal there is joy and confidence in movement, through play. **FUNdamentals** starts at 6. [S29] | Lots of play. Short structured bits. The off-track games (`08`) matter as much as track days. |
| World-class adult athletes did **more** other sports as children and **less** of their main sport. [S30] | Karting stays one activity among many. Weekdays are for other play and sports, not karting. |
| David Sera suggests about 2 practice sessions a week for ages **7–10**. Quality over volume. Never a chore. [S4] | Your two weekend days fit that, but your child is younger. So **8–10 runs is a maximum, not a target.** Stop early when attention fades. |
| The sim helps with precision and consistency. It doesn't replace the kart. [S14, S16] | Sim is a short "homework" session on Saturday evening that links Saturday's track focus to Sunday's. |
| AAP: for ages 2–5, about **1 hour a day** of screens, with a parent. No screens in the hour before bed. [S31] | Sim sessions are **10–15 minutes**, always with you, and they **count toward the 1 hour**. When your child turns 6, switch to "consistent limits". |
| Ages 3–5 need 10–13 hours of sleep. [S33] | Sim never goes late. Track days end before your child is wiped out. |
| iRacing requires users to be **13 or older**. [S18] | **No iRacing.** See `04-sim-sessions.md` for alternatives. |

### A special note: "lots of sim, little kart"

Your child has many hours on the sim and only one day in a real kart. That's a
strength: they know what a track and a steering wheel are. But some sim habits
**don't transfer** to a real kart, and some are risky in one [S14, S16, S17]:

- leaning on walls or cutting across grass with no consequence
- big, fast steering movements
- "I'll just restart" thinking after a mistake
- chasing a lap time or leaderboard instead of driving smoothly

Phase 1 is built to replace these habits with real-kart ones. The baseline check
(`00-assessment.md`) includes a "sim habits" section. [Synthesis]

### ID codes used everywhere
- `TR-xx` = track session card (`03`)
- `SIM-xx` = sim session card (`04`)
- `Dxx` = drill (`05`)
- `B / T / W / V / R` + number = cue (`06`): **B**rake, **T**hrottle, **W**heel (steering), **V**ision, **R**acecraft
- `Gxx` = off-track game (`08`)
- `Axx` = assessment item (`00`)
- `[Sxx]` = source (`10`) · `[Synthesis]` = coaching judgement, not a sourced fact

## 4. The weekend rhythm

```
SATURDAY                                  SUNDAY
──────────────────────────────            ──────────────────────────────
Safety check (09)          10 min         Safety check (09)          10 min
Track walk or game (D16/G) 10 min         Warm-up game (08)           5 min
MORNING: card runs 1–4,                   MORNING: card runs 1–4,
  + "your choice" run                       + "your choice" run
Lunch + free play          45+ min        Lunch + free play          45+ min
AFTERNOON: card "twist"                   AFTERNOON: card "twist"
  runs + "your choice" run                  runs + "your choice" run
End on a win + fun face                   End on a win + fun face
──────────────────────────────            Home. No karting talk unless
EVENING (optional): sim card              your child starts it.
10–15 min, ≥1 hour before bed
Links today's focus → tomorrow

MONDAY–FRIDAY: no planned karting. Normal life, other sports, free play.
Optional: one 5–10 min off-track game from 08 if your child asks for it.
```

Every **4th weekend is lighter**: one track day, or two "favourites" days with
fewer runs, plus the phase check. See `02-weekly-templates.md`.

## 5. Phases at a glance

| Phase | Guide weeks | In one line | Exit when… (see `01`) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Foundations | 1–4 | Driving the kart becomes automatic | Smooth pedals, stops on a mark, eyes up, knows flags, calm hands |
| 2. Confidence | 5–8 | Commit, and use the eyes | Full throttle to a mark, brakes before turning, waits for the turn, kart runs to the edge, the same with cones gone |
| 3. Consistency | 9–12 | The same good lap, again and again | Lap spread getting smaller (track and sim), calm in traffic, adapts to changes |

## 6. What changed from the first outline (and why)

- **Age 5 instead of 6–8:** shorter instructions, picture cards, more play, the
  Active Start stage [S29], and the AAP 2–5 screen guidance [S31].
- **Both weekend days on track:** each day uses one card (morning learn +
  afternoon twist). No weekday sessions.
- **Lots of sim experience:** Phase 1 adds "sim habit" swaps (see above).
- **Goals, other sports and personality weren't given.** I assumed: fun and
  confidence this year, maybe club racing later. Tell me if that's wrong and
  I'll adjust.

## 7. Safety in one line

Brakes are **never** disabled or restricted. The kart and gear are checked every
day. Sim torque is set low with an adult present. Full details in `09-safety.md`.
