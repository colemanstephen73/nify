# 01 · Phase Plan

Three phases: **Foundations → Confidence → Consistency.**
Each is *about* 4 weeks (4 weekends). **You move on when the exit criteria are
met, not when the weeks run out.** Staying longer in a phase is normal and fine.

Scores (1–3) and item IDs (A01…) come from `00-assessment.md`.
The weekend-by-weekend plan is in `02-weekly-templates.md`.

> **Rules in every phase**
> - One focus per session, at most 3 cues (`06`).
> - A child's choice in every session.
> - A fun face after every session. **Two drops in a row → `TR-00` Fun Only next time** (see README).
> - Safety items must be a **3** before moving on. No exceptions.

---

## Phase 1 · Foundations (guide: weekends 1–4)

**In one line:** driving the kart becomes automatic, so your child's brain has room for everything else.
Basic control should be automatic before technique is added. [Your brief; Synthesis]

**Goals**
1. Smooth pedals, feet take turns (no pressing both).
2. Stops on a chosen spot.
3. Eyes up and far. Rarely looks behind.
4. Knows the basic flags and the track's pit and spin rules.
5. Calm hands, smooth steering. Swaps sim habits for real-kart habits.

**Track cards (`03`)**
| Card | Focus | Main drill |
|---|---|---|
| TR-01 | Hello Kart (baseline + follow me) | D16, D03 |
| TR-02 | Sponge & Toothpaste pedals | D01 |
| TR-03 | Statue Stop | D02 |
| TR-04 | Pirate Telescope | D05 |
| TR-05 | Flags & Track Rules | D04 |
| TR-06 | Baby Bird Hands | D15 |

**Sim cards (`04`):** SIM-01 Set-up & Free Drive · SIM-02 Statue Stop · SIM-03 Gold Laps · SIM-04 Pirate Telescope
**Off-track (`08`):** G01–G06, G10, G11, G12

**Exit criteria. All must be true:**
- [ ] **Safety items at 3:** A01 (quiet foot in the pits), A03 (feet take turns), A05 (stops on a mark), A11 (4 flags), A13 (after a spin: hand up, stay put)
- [ ] **At 2 or more:** A02, A04, A06, A07, A08, A09, A25
- [ ] Fun average (A29) is **3 or more** over the last 4 sessions
- [ ] Your child is *asking* to go faster, or is already relaxed at their current pace [Synthesis]

**If stuck:** stay in Phase 1. Repeat the cards for items scoring 1. Add TR-00 days.
At 5, staying in Foundations for 8+ weekends is completely normal. [Synthesis]

## Phase 2 · Confidence (guide: weekends 5–8)

**In one line:** commit on the straights, wait for the corner, and let the eyes lead.

**Goals**
1. Full throttle up to a marker (commitment).
2. Brake (or lift) in a straight line, *then* turn.
3. **Wait for the turn-in point.** Turning in too early is the most common beginner mistake. [S6, S7]
4. Eyes: middle of the corner, then the exit kerb. Hands follow eyes. [S9 ⚠️, S10]
5. Let the kart roll to the track edge on exit. Calm hands. [S6, S9 ⚠️]
6. **Keep all of this when the cones are removed.** [S12, S13]

**Track cards**
| Card | Focus | Main drill |
|---|---|---|
| TR-07 | Flat Out to the Cone | D06 |
| TR-08 | Brake First, Then Turn | D07 |
| TR-09 | Wait for the Turn Cone | D08 |
| TR-10 | Eyes Through the Corner | D05 (exit version), D14 |
| TR-11 | Roll to the Edge | D09 |
| TR-12 | Cones on Holiday | D10 |

**Sim cards:** SIM-05 Flat Out to the Marker · SIM-06 Wait for the Turn · SIM-07 Roll to the Edge · SIM-08 Treasure Markers
**Off-track:** G03, G04, G07, G08, G09, plus multi-sport

**Exit criteria. All must be true:**
- [ ] **At 3:** A15 (brakes before turning), A16 (waits for the turn-in)
- [ ] **At 2 or more:** A10, A14, A17, A18 (skills hold with cones removed)
- [ ] All Phase 1 safety items still at 3
- [ ] Fun average 3 or more over the last 4 sessions

> Kid karts are slow. On some corners there's no real braking, just a lift.
> In that case A15 means "lifts in a straight line, then turns". Ask your club coach
> which corners need braking. [Synthesis]

**If stuck:** the most common block is fear when the D06 cone moves forward.
Move it **back**, praise the commitment, and spend a weekend on TR-10 and TR-11
(eyes and flow). Speed comes from those. [Synthesis]

## Phase 3 · Consistency (guide: weekends 9–12)

**In one line:** the same good lap, again and again, even when things change.

**Goals**
1. Laps more alike. Lap **spread** shrinks (spread = slowest lap − fastest lap). [S14, S16 ⚠️]
2. Same on the sim: 10-lap blocks judged on spread, not the fastest lap. [S16 ⚠️]
3. Can change line on purpose (varied practice). [Your brief; Synthesis]
4. Calm when another kart passes: "train on its track".
5. Copes with change: a new track, direction, weather, or time of day.

**Track cards**
| Card | Focus | Main drill |
|---|---|---|
| TR-13 | Grapes (same lap again) | D11 |
| TR-14 | Line Explorer | D12 |
| TR-15 | Traffic Buddy | D13 |
| TR-16 | Different Day, Same Lap | D11 + change |
| TR-17 | Show Me! (child teaches you) | Child's choice |

**Sim cards:** SIM-09 10-Lap Grapes · SIM-10 Line Explorer · SIM-11 Video Detective · SIM-12 Your Choice
**Off-track:** all games, more multi-sport

**Exit criteria. All must be true:**
- [ ] A19: track lap spread **smaller or the same** on 2 of the last 3 track days, compared with the first Phase 3 day
- [ ] A20: sim 10-lap spread smaller than in the first SIM-09 block, on 2 blocks
- [ ] **At 3:** A22 (calm when passed)
- [ ] **At 2 or more:** A21
- [ ] Fun average 3 or more, and your child **wants** to keep going

Your child is compared **only with themselves.** No lap-time targets, no comparison with other kids. [Synthesis]

## After Phase 3

Do the whole check (`00`). Then talk with your child: what was most fun?
Options [Synthesis]:
- **Keep going:** repeat Phase 3 at a new track, or in different conditions.
- **Club kid-kart events:** if your child asks. Check your series' Kid Kart age and gear rules first. Many start at age 5 [S26].
- **Back to Phase 1 cards** if there's been a long break. Skills come back fast.
- **A break from karting** if fun has dropped. Other sports build the athlete too. [S30]

## Signs it's too much (any phase) [Synthesis]

- Fun faces of 1–2, or two drops in a row → **TR-00**.
- "I don't want to go out" more than once in a day → stop the drills for the day.
- Tears, rubbing eyes, slower reactions late in the day → stop. Runs 8–10 are optional.
- Asking about the sim instead of the kart → the kart days may be too long or too drill-heavy.
