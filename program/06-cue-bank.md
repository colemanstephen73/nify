# 06 · Cue Bank

A **cue** is a few words you say to steer your child's attention.
Good cues for a 5-year-old are:

- **Pictures in the head** (analogies): "squeeze like a sponge". [S1]
- **About something outside the body** (external focus): "aim for the cone", not
  "move your arms". [S2]
- **Short.** Five words or fewer is ideal. [Synthesis]
- **Positive.** Say what to do, not what not to do ("eyes on the far cone",
  not "don't look down"). [Synthesis]

**Rules:** pick **1 main cue + up to 2 back-up cues per session** (3 at most).
Use the **same words every time.** Changing the wording makes it a new instruction.

Each cue has a **hand sign** so you can cue from the fence without shouting
(only where your track allows parents at the fence). Practise the signs in the paddock first.

## B · Braking

| ID | Say this | Picture / sign | What it teaches | Used in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **B1** | "Squeeze the brake like a sponge." | 🧽 · squeeze a fist slowly | Press the brake progressively, no stamping | TR-02, TR-03, TR-08, SIM-02, D01, G10 |
| **B2** | "Stop at the cone, like a statue." | 🗿 · flat palm, "stop" | Stopping on a chosen mark | TR-03, SIM-02, D02 |
| **B3** | "Brake first, then turn." | 🚂 · palm down, then a curving hand | Finish braking in a straight line before steering | TR-08, D07 |
| **B4** | "Let the sponge go slowly." | 🧽 · open the fist slowly | Release the brake smoothly | TR-08 |

## T · Throttle (go-pedal)

| ID | Say this | Picture / sign | What it teaches | Used in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **T1** | "Squeeze the go-pedal like toothpaste." | 🪥 · thumb pressing slowly | Smooth throttle application | TR-02, D01 |
| **T2** | "Foot to the floor until the cone." | 🦶⬇️ · stamp gently, point to the cone | Full commitment to a marker | TR-07, SIM-05, D06 |
| **T3** | "Wheel straight? Then go!" | ⬆️ · hands level, then thumbs up | Throttle as the steering unwinds [S11] | TR-11, SIM-07, D09 |
| **T4** | "Feet take turns." | 👣 · one hand up, the other down | Never press both pedals at once | TR-02, SIM-01, D01, A03 |
| **T5** | "Quiet foot in the pits." | 🤫 · finger to lips | Centrifugal clutch: the kart moves when revs rise [S24]. No throttle until the helper says "go". | Every track card (safety) |

## R · Racecraft and track sense

| ID | Say this | Picture / sign | What it teaches | Used in |

## Cues to avoid [Synthesis]

| Instead of… | Say… | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "Bend your elbows more." | W1 "baby bird" | Body-focused instructions tend to help less than external ones [S2] |
| "Don't look at the wall!" | V4 "Look at the gap." | Gives the eyes a target |
| "Go faster!" / "Push!" | One focus cue, e.g. T2 | "Faster" is a result, not something a child can *do* |
| "Brake later." | D07 cone + B3 | The cone does the explaining |
| Three new things on the grid | Nothing new. "Have fun, I'm watching!" | See `07-parent-playbook.md` |
