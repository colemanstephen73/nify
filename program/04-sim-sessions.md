# 04 · Sim Session Cards

**When:** Saturday evening, **10–15 minutes**, **at least 1 hour before bed**, with you sitting next to your child.
**Why so short:** for ages 2–5, the AAP suggests about **1 hour of screens a day**,
shared with a parent. The sim counts toward that hour. [S31]
**What the sim is for:** cheap, safe repetitions of today's track focus. Lorandi says the sim helps "partly" with
precision, consistency and spatial awareness. Real development happens in the kart. [S14]
**It is a supplement, never a substitute** for track time or play. [Your brief; S14, S16 ⚠️]

> **Before the first session, do the rig set-up in `09-safety.md` (section D) without your child there.**

---

## What the sim does and doesn't teach

| Transfers from the sim (use it for) | Doesn't transfer (don't expect it) |
|---|---|
| Learning a track layout and reference points [S14, S16 ⚠️] | Physical load: a real kart shakes, bumps, and is tiring [S16 ⚠️] |
| Consistency: repeating the same lap [S14] | Fear and consequence: a sim crash costs nothing [S16 ⚠️, S17] |
| Smooth pedal and steering habits | How a real kid kart actually slides and grips [S16 ⚠️] |
| Looking ahead, on triple screens | Real flags, marshals, pit routines |
| Data literacy, "grapes" (lap spread) | **Habits to unlearn:** leaning on walls [S14], cutting grass, restarting after mistakes [Synthesis] |

## Which sim software

| Software | Use? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Automobilista 2** (PC) | ✅ **Start here** | Has **rental karts** and a **GX390 race kart**, which are slower karts that are easier for a 5-year-old [S21]. Check the age rating on the store page. |
| **Kart Racing Pro** (PC) | ✅ Later (Phase 3, if your child copes) | Serious kart sim with a **Mini 60** class (still faster than a Kid Kart). **No AI opponents.** [S20] |
| **KartSim** (for rFactor 2) | ⚠️ Optional | Named in your brief. I couldn't check its current state. Try it only if it installs and runs well. |
| **Gran Turismo 7** | Only if you also own a PS5 | Console game. Your triple-monitor PC rig won't run it. |
| **iRacing** | ❌ **No** | Terms of use: users must be **13 or older**. [S18] |
| **KartKraft** | ❌ No | Development has stopped. No updates are planned. [S19] |

**Track:** pick **one short kart circuit** and keep it for a whole phase (familiar = calm).
If your home track exists in the sim, use it. Change tracks in Phase 3 for variety. [Synthesis]

## Every sim session follows the same shape [Synthesis]

```
1. Fit check (1 min)    → `09` section D: feet reach, fingers clear, torque low
2. The card's game      → 8–10 min
3. Your Choice (2 min)  → free drive, or a favourite game from an earlier card
4. "The sim goes to sleep" → you both press the power button. Fun face 1–5.
```
- Use a **sand timer or visual timer** your child can see. When it runs out, the sim sleeps. No arguments, because the timer decided.
- **Hands off the wheel = pause the game.** (Safety: a direct-drive wheel can spin when let go. See `09`.)
- **No leaderboards and no "beat your best" talk.** Chasing a leaderboard teaches over-driving. [S16 ⚠️]
- Lap times only appear as **grapes** (spread), never as "fastest". [S16 ⚠️]

## Phase 1

### SIM-01 · Set-up & Free Drive
- **Picture strip:** 🪑 · 🦶🦶 · 🐦 · 🏎️
- **Goal:** your child fits the rig safely, and you **watch** their current sim habits.
- **Track:** your chosen phase track · **Kart:** slowest rental kart (AMS2)
- **Cues (max 3):** **T4** "Feet take turns" · W1 "Baby bird hands"
- **Exercise / game:**
  1. Fit check (`09`). Check the brake: can your child reach full brake with a firm squeeze? (Look at the input bar if your sim shows one, or your Fanatec's brake level readout.) [S23]
  2. **Pedal game in the pits:** you say 🧽 or 🪥, they squeeze. Same as D01.
  3. **Free drive, 5 minutes.** You say nothing. Just watch.
- **Scoring (for you, not them):** A23 (walls or grass used?), A24 (restarts after mistakes?), A03 (feet take turns?).
- **Time cap:** 15 min.
- **Links to track:** tomorrow is **TR-02 Sponge & Toothpaste**. Say: "Tomorrow your feet will do the sponge and toothpaste in the real kart."

### SIM-02 · Statue Stop
- **Picture strip:** 🗿 · 🪧 · 🧽
- **Goal:** stop next to a chosen feature, every time.
- **Track:** phase track · **Kart:** rental kart
- **Cues:** **B2** "Stop at the cone, like a statue" · B1 "Squeeze like a sponge"
- **Exercise / game:** **D18 Sim Stop Box.** Pick 3 features (a sign, a kerb end, a tyre wall). You point at the screen to show which one. Your child drives one lap and stops next to it. Repeat with a different feature.
- **Scoring:** stops next to the feature: 1 = past it, 2 = near it, 3 = right next to it. Best 3 of 5.
- **Time cap:** 12 min.
- **Links to track:** tomorrow (**TR-04**), run 1 is a statue stop warm-up.

### SIM-03 · Gold Laps (no touching)
- **Picture strip:** ⭐ · 🚫🧱 · 🌈
- **Goal:** swap sim habits (walls, grass) for real-kart ones: clean, smooth laps. [S14]
- **Track:** phase track · **Kart:** rental kart
- **Cues:** **W3** "Paint a smooth rainbow" · V4 "Look at the gap, not the wall" · W1 "Baby bird hands"
- **Exercise / game:** **D19 No-Touch Laps.** A lap with no wall, grass or barrier touch = ⭐ gold lap. How many gold laps before the timer ends?
- **Scoring:** count gold laps. Compare only with your child's last Gold Laps session.
- **Time cap:** 12 min.
- **Links to track:** tomorrow (**TR-05**), run 1 is "baby bird hands".

### SIM-04 · Pirate Telescope (lighter weekend, optional)
- **Picture strip:** 🏴‍☠️🔭 · 🖥️🖥️🖥️
- **Goal:** eyes up and far. Use the side screens of the triple set-up to look into corners early.
- **Track:** phase track · **Kart:** rental kart
- **Cues:** **V1** "Pirate telescope: look far!" · V5 "At the turn, look at the exit"
- **Exercise / game:** while driving, you ask "What's at the end of this straight?" or "What colour is the kerb at the next corner?" Your child answers *before* they get there.
- **Scoring:** correct early answers out of 5.
- **Time cap:** 10 min.
- **Links to track:** leads into Phase 2's eyes work (**TR-10**).

## Phase 2

### SIM-05 · Flat Out to the Marker
- **Picture strip:** 🦶⬇️ · 🪧 · 🏁
- **Goal:** full throttle to a chosen feature.
- **Track:** phase track · **Kart:** rental kart (or GX390 if confident)
- **Cues:** **T2** "Foot to the floor until the cone" · V1 "Pirate telescope"
- **Exercise / game:** pick a feature on one straight as the "cone". 2 laps at that spot, then your child **chooses** whether to move it closer (same idea as D06).
- **Scoring:** laps held flat to the marker out of 5.
- **Time cap:** 12 min.
- **Links to track:** tomorrow (**TR-08**), run 1 is "flat out to the cone".

### SIM-06 · Wait for the Turn
- **Picture strip:** ⏳ · 🪧 · ↪️
- **Goal:** turn in at a chosen feature, not early. [S6, S7]
- **Track:** phase track · **Kart:** rental or GX390
- **Cues:** **W4** "Wait for the turn cone" · V2 "Middle of the corner, then the exit kerb"
- **Exercise / game:** choose a turn-in feature for 2 corners. Each lap: "wait… turn!" Your child says "now!" out loud when they turn. You'll hear whether it's on time.
- **Scoring:** on-time turns out of 6 (3 laps × 2 corners).
- **Time cap:** 12 min.
- **Links to track:** tomorrow (**TR-10**), run 1 is "wait for the turn".

### SIM-07 · Roll to the Edge
- **Picture strip:** ⚽➡️ · ⬆️ · 🦶
- **Goal:** let the kart run out to the edge on exit, with throttle as the wheel straightens. [S6, S11]
- **Track:** phase track · **Kart:** rental or GX390
- **Cues:** **W5** "Let it roll to the edge" · T3 "Wheel straight? Then go!"
- **Exercise / game:** pick 2 corners. If the kart rolls out to the exit kerb smoothly, your child shouts "BALL!" You count the balls.
- **Scoring:** balls out of 6.
- **Time cap:** 12 min.
- **Links to track:** tomorrow (**TR-12 Cones on Holiday**), run 1 is "roll to the edge".

### SIM-08 · Treasure Markers (lighter weekend, optional)
- **Picture strip:** 🔎 · 🪧 · 🧭
- **Goal:** find and use real features (no cones in the sim anyway) for flat-out, brake, turn-in and exit. Same idea as D10.
- **Track:** a **new** short track (variety)
- **Cues:** **V3** "Hands follow eyes" · V5 "At the turn, look at the exit"
- **Exercise / game:** 2 slow laps: your child names a treasure for each job at one corner. Then 4 laps using them.
- **Scoring:** treasures found and used (out of 4 jobs).
- **Time cap:** 12 min.
- **Links to track:** Phase 3 starts with **TR-13 Grapes**, which uses the same treasures.

## Phase 3

### SIM-09 · 10-Lap Grapes
- **Picture strip:** 🍇 · 🔟 · 🔁
- **Goal:** consistency. 10 laps judged on **spread, not the fastest lap**. [S16 ⚠️, S14]
- **Track:** phase track · **Kart:** GX390, or Kart Racing Pro's slowest kart if your child is ready [S20]
- **Cues:** **V5** "At the turn, look at the exit" · + best cue
- **Exercise / game:** **D17.** 10 laps in a row, **no restarts**. You write the lap times and mark "oops" laps.
- **Scoring:** spread = slowest − fastest. Show it as grapes: fewer gaps = a tighter bunch. Compare only with the last SIM-09.
- **Time cap:** 15 min. On a short track, 10 laps take most of it. If not, do 6 laps.
- **Links to track:** tomorrow (**TR-14**), run 1 uses V5. In week 11, compare with **TR-13**'s track grapes.

### SIM-10 · Line Explorer
- **Picture strip:** 🐋 · 🐟 · 🦀
- **Goal:** drive three lines on purpose through one corner (D12).
- **Track:** phase track · **Kart:** GX390
- **Cues:** **V2** "Middle, then the exit kerb" · W5 "Roll to the edge"
- **Exercise / game:** 1 lap each: whale (wide), fish (middle), crab (tight). Then your child picks the best one and drives it for 3 laps.
- **Scoring:** lines shown on request out of 3 (same as A21).
- **Time cap:** 12 min.
- **Links to track:** tomorrow (**TR-16 Different Day**) asks your child to adapt. Changing line on purpose is practice for that.

### SIM-11 · Video Detective (replay)
- **Picture strip:** 🎬 · 🔎 · ⭐
- **Goal:** your child watches their own replay and finds **one good thing**.
- **Track:** any
- **Cues:** none. Your child does the talking.
- **Exercise / game:** 5 minutes of driving, then 3 minutes of replay (the sim's own replay). "Find one place you were smooth." "Where were your eyes?"
- **Scoring:** none. Write down what they spotted.
- **Time cap:** 10 min.
- **Links to track:** next track day, use the thing they spotted as the main cue.

### SIM-12 · Your Choice
- **Picture strip:** 🎉 · 👉 · 🏎️
- **Goal:** fun and ownership. Your child picks any earlier sim game (show the picture strips).
- **Cues:** that card's cues
- **Time cap:** 12 min.
- **Links to track:** a celebration of the program. Ask what they'd like to practise next.

## Logging sim sessions

In the tracker: `session_type = sim`, `focus` = the card (e.g. `SIM-09 10-Lap Grapes`),
`drill` = D17/D18/D19 or the card's game, `score` = the card's score, `fun_rating` = face, `notes` = spread, software, kart.
