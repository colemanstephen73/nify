# 02 · Weekly Templates

Built for **your inputs:** weekends only · track on **both** days · up to **8–10
runs a day** · sim on Saturday evening · nothing planned Monday–Friday.

> **8–10 runs is a maximum, not a target.** Your child is 5. Stop when attention
> fades, even after run 4. A short, happy day beats a long, tired one. [S4, Synthesis]

---

## The standard weekend

### Saturday
| Time | What | File |
|---|---|---|
| Arrive | Kart + gear safety check (10 min) | `09` |
| | Track walk treasure hunt (closed track) **or** a warm-up game | D16 / `08` |
| Morning | **Today's card: runs 1–4** (one drill each) | `03` |
| | **Run 5: Your Choice** (child picks a game-run or free run) | `03` |
| Lunch | Eat, drink, **free play (45+ min).** No karting talk. | `07` |
| Afternoon | **Card "Afternoon Twist": runs 6–9** (same focus, varied) | `03` |
| | **Run 10: Your Choice** (optional) | |
| End | Finish on a good run. Fun face. One "best thing". | `07` |
| Evening (optional) | **Sim card, 10–15 min**, ≥1 hour before bed, with you | `04` |

### Sunday
Same as Saturday, with the next card. Its **run 1 is always a "yesterday's cue"
warm-up run** (repeat yesterday's main cue once). Practising old skills
between new ones helps them stick. [Synthesis]
No sim on Sunday evening (school night, sleep comes first [S33]).

### Monday–Friday
- **No planned karting or sim.**
- Normal play, other sports, outdoor time. Aim for plenty of active play every day. [S34]
- Optional: one 5–10 minute game from `08` **if your child asks**.

### Screen budget (both days)
The sim card **plus** TV, tablet and video review together stay at about
**1 hour a day** while your child is 5. No screens in the hour before bed. [S31]

## Lighter weekends (4, 8, 12)

At the end of each phase. [Synthesis]
- **One track day only** (or two short days with **max 6 runs each**).
- Morning: favourite drills (the child picks from the cards they've done).
- Afternoon: **phase check.** Watch and score with `00-assessment.md`.
- Other day: a different sport or a family outing. No sim needed.

## 12-week plan

**How to read it:** "Plan" is the card if things go as expected.
**Gate** = before using a new card, check the previous card's skill is at **2 or more**.
If not, **repeat the previous card** (or use TR-00 if fun dropped). The weeks slide; that's normal.

## When things change

| Situation | What to do [Synthesis] |
|---|---|
| **Fun dropped two sessions in a row** (or two 1–2s in a row) | Next session = **TR-00 Fun Only.** Then repeat the last card you finished. |
| Only one track day this weekend | Do Saturday's card. Skip Sunday's. Don't squeeze two cards into one day. |
| Rain | If the track runs in the wet and your child is keen: TR-00 or the current card with **half the runs**. Wet is great *variety*, but only if it's fun. |
| Track session is shorter (fewer runs) | Do runs 1–4 of the card. Drop the twist. |
| Your child is ill or tired | No karting. No sim. Rest. |
| Your child asks for *more* | Great, but keep the caps (10 runs, 15 sim minutes). Leave them wanting more. |
| A race or club event comes up | Use `07-parent-playbook.md` race-day scripts. No new drills that weekend. |
