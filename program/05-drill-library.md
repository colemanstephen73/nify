# 05 · Drill Library

Every drill used in the session cards is here. Each one has a purpose, setup,
cues, progressions, common mistakes and safety notes.
Cue IDs (like `B1`) are in `06-cue-bank.md`.

> **Cone rules for every drill (read once)** [Synthesis]
> - Only use cones where **your track allows it**. Ask the track manager. Private or quiet sessions are best.
> - Use **soft, light** cones (or flat marker discs). Put them **at the track edge**, never in the kart's path.
> - Place or move cones **only when no karts are moving**, and with the track's permission.
> - **Remove cones after 2–3 track days** of the same drill, then use real track features (kerb ends, signs, cracks, posts). Drivers who fixate on cones can feel lost without them. [S12, S13]

> **Brakes are never disabled, restricted or "taped" to make a drill work.** [Your brief; Synthesis]

## Phase 1 drills

### D01 · Paddock Pedal Game (engine OFF)
- **Purpose:** make pedal control automatic before driving. Smooth squeeze, feet take turns.
- **Setup:** kart on a stand or on flat ground, **engine off**, wheels chocked. Child sits in the kart. You stand in front holding picture cards: 🧽 (brake) and 🪥 (go).
- **How:** show a card. Child squeezes that pedal. "Slow squeeze… hold… let go slowly." Then switch cards fast and slow. 10–20 calls, 2 minutes.
- **Cues:** B1, T1, T4
- **Progressions:** (1) slow calls → (2) fast calls → (3) child closes eyes and you *say* the colour → (4) child calls and *you* do it on your hands (role swap = choice).
- **Common mistakes:** pressing both pedals together; stamping. Fix: slow down the calls. Praise "feet took turns!"
- **Safety:** engine off, kill switch off, wheels chocked. Fingers away from the chain and sprocket.

### D02 · Stop Box
- **Purpose:** stop on a chosen spot, every time. A safety skill and the start of braking-marker skill.
- **Setup:** on a straight (or the pit lane if allowed), make a "box" with 4 soft cones at the edge. The box is about 3 kart lengths long. [Synthesis]
- **How:** drive in slowly and stop with the kart's nose inside the box. Start at walking speed, then a bit faster each time.
- **Cues:** B2, B1
- **Score:** 1 = past the box, 2 = in the box after a correction, 3 = smooth stop in the box.
- **Progressions:** make the box shorter → faster entry → no cones, stop next to a real feature ("the white post").
- **Common mistakes:** stamping the brake (sliding) → go back to B1; stopping early → that's fine, praise it.
- **Safety:** only when the track is closed to other karts, or in the pit lane with permission.

### D03 · Follow the Leader
- **Purpose:** learn a line and smooth speed by copying. The brain copies before it can explain.
- **Setup — choose what your track allows:**
  - **(a) Lead kart:** an adult, coach or experienced older driver drives ahead at a gentle, steady speed. **Only in a session the track approves for mixed karts.**
  - **(b) On foot ("Walk the Line"):** walk or jog the racing line before the session. Your child follows you holding a paper-plate steering wheel (game G12). No kart needed.
  - **(c) Sim:** see SIM-01 and SIM-11.
- **Cues:** R1, V1
- **Score:** 1 = lost the leader / looked around a lot, 2 = followed most of the lap, 3 = full lap behind the leader, eyes forward.
- **Progressions:** slow → normal practice pace → the leader takes a *slightly different* line and the child copies (variety).
- **Common mistakes:** child looks back for the leader after being dropped → R1 "your lap is in front of you". The leader keeps a big gap (several kart lengths) and never brakes suddenly. [Synthesis]
- **Safety:** the leader must be a calm, predictable driver. No overtaking during this drill.

### D04 · Flag Game
- **Purpose:** know the flags without thinking.
- **Setup:** flag picture cards (🟩 🟨 🟥 🏁) or real flags. In the paddock first.
- **How (paddock):** you show a flag, child does the action: 🟩 drive the plate wheel fast, 🟨 slow snail, 🟥 freeze like a statue, 🏁 cheer. (Same as game G02.)
- **How (track):** only where your track allows parents or marshals to show flags at a safe, fenced spot.
- **Cues:** R2
- **Score:** correct actions out of 4.
- **Progressions:** mix the order → faster → add your track's other flags when your child is ready (check the club's flag list).
- **Common mistakes:** stopping *dead* on the track for yellow → yellow means slow, not stop.
- **Safety:** official marshals' flags always come first.

### D05 · Pirate Telescope (eyes far)
- **Purpose:** eyes up and far, not on the front of the kart.
- **Setup:** at the far end of the main straight, behind the barrier and outside the track, put something bright and changeable: a coloured towel, a large picture card, a parent in a hat. Only where spectators are allowed.
- **How:** each lap, or each run, change the colour or picture. After the run, ask: "What did the pirate see?"
- **Cues:** V1, V4
- **Score:** 1 = didn't see it, 2 = saw it sometimes, 3 = saw every change.
- **Progressions:** put the target further round the corner → two targets in different places.
- **Common mistakes:** child stares at the target and drifts toward it → move the target to the *exit* side of a straight, so "looking at it" means looking down the track.
- **Safety:** the target person stands in a spectator area only. Never on the track side of the barrier.

### D15 · Calm Hands
- **Purpose:** relaxed grip, small smooth steering. (Sera judges progress partly by calm hands. [S9 ⚠️])
- **Setup:** nothing on track. Off track: put a sticker at 9 and 3 on the wheel (if allowed) or on your child's gloves.
- **How:** "Baby bird hands" for one run. Watch from the fence or on video: are the hands quiet on the straights? Smooth in corners?
- **Cues:** W1, W2, W3
- **Score:** 1 = gripping or sawing at the wheel, 2 = calm on straights, 3 = calm everywhere.
- **Progressions:** in the paddock, play "Mirror Me" (G11) → track runs → video check (D14).
- **Common mistakes:** "calm" becomes floppy → the bird mustn't fly away. Firm, not tight.
- **Safety:** none extra.

### D16 · Track Walk Treasure Hunt
- **Purpose:** learn the track and find reference points (things to look at) before driving.
- **Setup:** walk the track **only when it is closed** and the track allows it.
- **How:** walk the line together. Give each corner a fun name ("Dinosaur Corner"). Look for treasures: a kerb's colour, a sign, a crack, a post. Take a photo of each corner. That's your picture map for between-run chats (`07`).
- **Cues:** V2 (point at the middle, then the exit)
- **Progressions:** your child leads the walk and points out the treasures.
- **Safety:** closed track only. Hold hands near the track edge.

## Phase 2 drills

### D06 · Flat Out Until the Cone
- **Purpose:** full commitment on the straight, up to a marker.
- **Setup:** one soft cone at the track edge, **well before** a corner's braking area. On day 1, put it where your child already lifts off easily.
- **How:** "Foot to the floor until the cone, then do your normal corner." After every 2 good runs, **move the cone a little closer** to the corner (about one kart length). [Synthesis]
- **Cues:** T2, V1
- **Score:** 1 = lifts before the cone, 2 = holds it some laps, 3 = holds it every lap and then slows calmly.
- **Progressions:** cone moves step by step toward the normal braking point → then it is removed and a real feature is used (D10).
- **Common mistakes:** braking hard and late after the cone (panic) → move the cone back one step. Being scared is information, not failure.
- **Safety:** **never** move the cone past where your child can comfortably slow for the corner. Moving it back is always OK.

### D07 · Brake at the Cone
- **Purpose:** braking at a marker, in a straight line, before turning.
- **Setup:** one soft cone at the track edge where braking should **start**. (On kid-kart corners this is often just a lift. Ask your coach or club.)
- **How:** "Squeeze the brake at the cone, finish, then turn."
- **Cues:** B3, B1, (B4)
- **Score:** 1 = no clear braking point, 2 = brakes at the cone but turns while braking, 3 = brakes at the cone, finishes, then turns.
- **Progressions:** after 2–3 days, swap the cone for a real feature (D10). Later, a second cone at the turn point (combine with D08).
- **Common mistakes:** stamping → B1. Braking while turning → B3, and move the cone a little earlier.
- **Safety:** never restrict the brakes. Soft cones at the edge only.

### D08 · Wait for the Turn Cone
- **Purpose:** stop the most common beginner error, **turning in too early**. [S6, S7]
- **Setup:** one soft cone on the outside edge, where the kart should **start** turning.
- **How:** "Keep the kart straight… wait… turn at the cone."
- **Cues:** W4, V2
- **Score:** 1 = turns before the cone most laps, 2 = waits some laps, 3 = turns at the cone every lap.
- **Progressions:** cone removed → use a real feature (D10).
- **Common mistakes:** waits, then turns sharply → W3 "smooth rainbow".
- **Safety:** cone on the outside edge, off the racing surface.

### D09 · Roll to the Edge
- **Purpose:** use the whole track on exit. The kart runs naturally to the outside edge as the wheel straightens. (Sera judges progress by the kart running to the edge on exit. [S6, S9 ⚠️])
- **Setup:** one cone (or a parent behind the barrier holding a picture) at the **exit** edge.
- **How:** "Look at the exit cone, let the kart roll out to it. Wheel straight, then go."
- **Cues:** W5, T3, V2
- **Score:** 1 = stays tight on the inside, 2 = sometimes uses the edge, 3 = smooth run to the edge every lap.
- **Progressions:** cone removed → exit kerb is the target.
- **Common mistakes:** steering *toward* the edge (wiggle) → "let it roll, don't steer it."
- **Safety:** exit cone placed outside the kerb, never on the track.

### D10 · Cones Away
- **Purpose:** keep the skill when the cones are gone, using real track features. [S12, S13]
- **Setup:** take away the cones from D06–D09. On a track walk (D16), pick a real "treasure" for each cone's job.
- **How:** "Today the cones are on holiday. Use the yellow post instead."
- **Cues:** V2, V3, plus the cue of the skill you're keeping
- **Score:** same scale as the drill being replaced.
- **Common mistakes:** the skill drops a little at first → that's normal. Don't put the cones back unless it drops for a whole day.

### D14 · Video Detective
- **Purpose:** your child sees their own driving and spots one good thing.
- **Setup:** an onboard camera **securely mounted on the kart** (many series ban helmet cameras, so check), or a phone filming from a spectator spot. Drones only where the track allows and local law permits, never over people. [Synthesis]
- **How:** show **one or two clips, under 2 minutes**. Ask first: "What do you see?" Then: "Find one place you were smooth." (Counts as screen time. [S31])
- **Progressions:** compare two clips: "Which one has calmer hands?"
- **Common mistakes:** the parent talks through the whole video → let your child talk first.

## Phase 3 drills

### D11 · Same Lap Again
- **Purpose:** consistency. Make the laps more alike.
- **Setup:** time laps with the track's timing, a lap timer, or a phone stopwatch.
- **How:** "Can you make every lap the same, like stickers in a row?" After the run, write the lap times. **Spread** = slowest lap minus fastest lap. Tell your child "your stickers were close together!" (a bunch of grapes 🍇), not the numbers.
- **Cues:** V5, plus the child's best cue from Phase 2
- **Score:** compare your child **only with themselves**: 3 = spread smaller than last time, 2 = about the same, 1 = bigger. [Synthesis]
- **Progressions:** 3-lap blocks → 5-lap blocks → a whole run.
- **Common mistakes:** chasing one fast lap → praise the *grapes*, not the fastest lap.
- **Safety:** none extra.

### D12 · Line Explorer
- **Purpose:** varied practice. Try a different line on purpose to learn what the kart does. Variety helps long-term learning even if it looks messier. [Your brief; Synthesis]
- **Setup:** on a track walk, choose one corner. Name three lines: **"wide like a whale" 🐋, "middle like a fish" 🐟, "tight like a crab" 🦀**.
- **How:** one lap each, in order, then the child picks their favourite for the rest of the run.
- **Cues:** V2, W5
- **Score:** 3 = could drive all three on request, 2 = two of three, 1 = one.
- **Common mistakes:** the tight line becomes a kerb-hit → tight means close, not on top.
- **Safety:** never use lines that point the kart toward a barrier.

### D13 · Traffic Buddy
- **Purpose:** calm in traffic. Hold a steady line while another kart passes.
- **Setup:** **only** in a session with other drivers your track approves (club practice day, kid-kart session). Agree with the other driver's parent or coach first.
- **How:** "If someone comes past, be a train on its track." Afterwards: "Did anyone pass you? What did you do?"
- **Cues:** R3, R1
- **Score:** 1 = swerved or stopped, 2 = wobbled but OK, 3 = held the line calmly.
- **Common mistakes:** looking behind all the time → R1. Moving over suddenly to "help" → the train stays on its track.
- **Safety:** the track's own passing and blue-flag rules always come first.

## Sim-only drills (used in `04-sim-sessions.md`)

### D17 · 10-Lap Grapes (sim consistency block)
- **Purpose:** consistency, judged on **spread, not hot laps**. [S16 ⚠️, S14]
- **Setup:** a short kart track, an easy kart (see `04`). Write the lap times.
- **How:** 10 laps in a row. Count all laps, and put an "oops" mark next to laps with an off-track. **Spread = slowest − fastest.**
- **Score:** child vs their own last block. Celebrate a smaller spread with a grape sticker 🍇.
- **Common mistakes:** restarting after a mistake → no restarts in a block. Keep going like in a real kart.
- **Safety:** see sim rules in `09`.

### D18 · Sim Stop Box
- Same as D02, but use a real feature in the sim (a sign, a kerb end, a tyre stack) as the box. Cue B2.

### D19 · No-Touch Laps
- **Purpose:** remove sim habits that don't work in a real kart: leaning on walls, cutting across grass. Lorandi describes leaning on walls on a home sim as a kid as a habit "not … with a future". [S14]
- **How:** a lap only counts as a "gold lap" ⭐ if it has no wall, grass or barrier touch. How many gold laps in 5 minutes?
- **Cues:** V4, W3
- **Common mistakes:** going very slowly to avoid touches → that's OK at first. Smooth beats fast.
